1. Christie, F. M. (1996). *Urban agriculture in Maputo*. MA, Wits. http://hdl.handle.net/10539/22540 (scan com OCR ruim).
   - Cooperativas: 1980 7 coop./500 membros/4 t carne; 1981 24/1 177/171,5 ha/148 t culturas/6 t carne;
     1982 33/2 137; 1984 151/~8,3 mil/471 ha (cap. 3, ~pp. 22–23; 7.2).
